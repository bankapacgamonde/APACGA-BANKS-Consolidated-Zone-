# APACGAMONDE Bonds Framework

APACGAMONDE Bonds is a compliance-first framework for defining, approving, issuing, monitoring, and reporting bond-related products or programs within the APACGAMONDE consolidated banking zone.

## Purpose

This framework supports bond initiatives by ensuring that each proposed instrument is documented, risk-rated, legally reviewed, operationally controlled, and monitored throughout its lifecycle.

## Scope

The framework applies to bond-related activities including:

- Sovereign, supranational, municipal, corporate, covered, secured, unsecured, green, social, sustainability, and private placement bonds.
- Primary issuance, secondary market distribution, custody, settlement, and investor reporting workflows.
- Internal approval, compliance review, treasury management, and audit oversight processes.

## Bond Legitimization Protocol

1. **Product Definition** — Document issuer, currency, tenor, coupon structure, repayment profile, collateral, guarantees, use of proceeds, and investor eligibility.
2. **Regulatory Classification** — Identify applicable securities, banking, tax, listing, disclosure, and cross-border distribution requirements.
3. **Risk Assessment** — Score credit, market, liquidity, interest-rate, foreign-exchange, operational, legal, sanctions, conduct, and reputational risks.
4. **Compliance Mapping** — Align controls with FATF, Basel, IFRS, securities-market rules, AML/CFT obligations, sanctions screening, and local laws.
5. **Disclosure Review** — Validate offering memoranda, term sheets, investor notices, risk factors, and marketing materials before use.
6. **Approval Workflow** — Obtain sign-off from treasury, risk, legal, compliance, finance, operations, and audit stakeholders.
7. **Operational Readiness** — Confirm ISIN or identifier setup, settlement instructions, custody arrangements, payment agents, accounting treatment, and audit trails.
8. **Investor Protection Controls** — Verify suitability, eligibility, fair dealing, conflicts-of-interest disclosures, complaint handling, and recordkeeping.
9. **Lifecycle Monitoring** — Track covenants, coupon payments, redemptions, credit events, rating actions, proceeds allocation, and regulatory reporting.
10. **Annual Review** — Reassess controls, performance, exceptions, and regulatory changes at least once per year.

## Minimum Documentation Pack

Each bond initiative should maintain the following records:

- Bond product approval memo.
- Term sheet and final offering documentation.
- Risk assessment and mitigation plan.
- Compliance checklist and legal opinion or review note.
- Investor eligibility and suitability evidence.
- Accounting, custody, settlement, and payment procedures.
- Ongoing monitoring reports and exception logs.
- Audit review report and annual refresh record.

## Control Principles

- No bond product should be launched without documented legal, compliance, risk, and treasury approval.
- All investor-facing statements must be accurate, balanced, and approved before distribution.
- Proceeds usage, covenant performance, and payment obligations must be traceable through reliable records.
- Exceptions must be escalated, remediated, and retained for audit review.

## Review Cadence

- **Pre-launch:** Full product, risk, legal, and operational approval.
- **Monthly:** Payment, covenant, exception, and investor complaint monitoring.
- **Quarterly:** Portfolio exposure, market-risk, liquidity, and compliance reporting.
- **Annually:** End-to-end framework review and refresh.
