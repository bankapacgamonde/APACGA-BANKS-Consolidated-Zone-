# APACGAWORLD Bonds

APACGAWORLD Bonds are proposed debt instruments issued, arranged, or administered under the APACGAMONDE compliance framework. This document provides an initial governance and compliance baseline for designing, approving, issuing, monitoring, and reporting bond programs.

## Purpose

- Define a controlled process for APACGAWORLD bond issuance and administration.
- Align bond activity with banking, securities, accounting, and anti-financial-crime expectations.
- Create auditable records for each issuance, investor communication, and lifecycle event.

## Scope

This baseline applies to the following bond-related activities:

1. Program authorization and mandate approval.
2. Term sheet and prospectus preparation.
3. Investor onboarding, sanctions screening, and suitability checks.
4. Pricing, allocation, settlement, coupon payments, redemptions, and buybacks.
5. Ongoing disclosure, financial reporting, covenant monitoring, and audit review.

## Governance Requirements

- Obtain board or delegated committee approval before launching a bond program.
- Appoint accountable owners for treasury, legal, compliance, risk, finance, operations, and audit.
- Maintain a document register for approvals, offering materials, investor records, payment evidence, and regulatory filings.
- Segregate duties across origination, approval, settlement, reconciliation, and reporting.
- Review each issuance against applicable securities, banking, tax, and foreign-exchange rules before market launch.

## Minimum Compliance Controls

| Control Area | Minimum Requirement | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Know Your Customer | Identify and verify eligible investors before allocation. | KYC files, screening logs, approval notes |
| Sanctions and AML | Screen investors, intermediaries, beneficial owners, and payment routes. | Screening results, exception approvals |
| Securities Compliance | Confirm registration, exemption, private placement, or listing requirements. | Legal memo, regulator correspondence |
| Risk Management | Assess credit, liquidity, interest-rate, market, operational, and legal risks. | Risk assessment, committee minutes |
| Accounting | Define recognition, measurement, coupon accrual, and fair-value approach. | Accounting memo, IFRS mapping |
| Audit Trail | Preserve immutable records of approvals, transactions, and reconciliations. | Logs, reconciliations, audit reports |

## Bond Lifecycle Checklist

### 1. Pre-Issuance

- Prepare issuance proposal, use-of-proceeds statement, and risk assessment.
- Complete compliance mapping against FATF, Basel, IFRS, and local regulatory requirements.
- Obtain legal, tax, treasury, finance, and audit sign-off.
- Approve final term sheet, offering circular, or private placement memorandum.

### 2. Issuance and Settlement

- Confirm investor eligibility and completed onboarding.
- Validate pricing, allocation, settlement instructions, and payment confirmations.
- Record the liability, issuance costs, and related disclosures in the finance system.
- Reconcile proceeds received against allocation records and bank statements.

### 3. Post-Issuance Monitoring

- Track coupon dates, principal maturity, covenants, and reporting deadlines.
- Monitor investor communications and regulatory disclosure obligations.
- Reconcile paying-agent, registrar, custody, and bank records.
- Escalate covenant breaches, payment delays, sanctions hits, or investor complaints.

### 4. Maturity or Redemption

- Confirm redemption funding, payment instructions, tax treatment, and approvals.
- Reconcile principal and coupon payments after settlement.
- Archive final lifecycle records and complete a post-issuance audit review.

## Annual Review

At least annually, compliance and internal audit should review the APACGAWORLD Bonds control environment, test a sample of bond records, assess regulatory changes, and recommend updates to this baseline.
