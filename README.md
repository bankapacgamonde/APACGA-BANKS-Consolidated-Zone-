## APACGA Currencies

APACGA Currencies defines a consolidated currency governance framework for APACGAMONDE banking, treasury, settlement, and compliance operations across Asia-Pacific and Greater Africa corridors.

### Core Currency Principles

1. **ISO 4217 Standardization** — Record every fiat currency using its official three-letter ISO 4217 code.
2. **Central-Bank Alignment** — Validate currency usage, settlement rules, and reporting obligations against the relevant monetary authority.
3. **FX Risk Controls** — Apply daily rate validation, exposure limits, and maker-checker approvals for foreign-exchange activity.
4. **Sanctions & AML Screening** — Screen currency flows, counterparties, originators, and beneficiaries before settlement.
5. **Auditability** — Maintain immutable records for rates, trade confirmations, approvals, reconciliations, and exception handling.
6. **Liquidity Management** — Monitor nostro/vostro balances, cut-off times, and settlement cycles by currency.
7. **Regulatory Reporting** — Map transaction data to FATF, Basel, IFRS, OHADA, local tax, and central-bank reporting requirements.

### Operating Currency Register

The APACGA operating currency register should include, at minimum:

- Currency code and legal tender name
- Issuing jurisdiction and central bank
- Settlement network or correspondent bank
- Cut-off time and settlement cycle
- Daily reference-rate source
- AML, sanctions, and capital-control notes
- Treasury owner and compliance reviewer
- Last review date and next review date

### Currency Onboarding Protocol

1. **Business Justification** — Document expected volumes, customers, jurisdictions, and use cases.
2. **Legal Review** — Confirm the currency is permitted for the proposed products and customer segments.
3. **Risk Assessment** — Score FX, liquidity, sanctions, AML, tax, and operational risks.
4. **Correspondent Setup** — Establish approved settlement routes, accounts, and reconciliation procedures.
5. **System Configuration** — Configure currency code, decimal precision, rate feeds, ledgers, fees, and limits.
6. **Pilot Settlement** — Run controlled test settlements and exception scenarios.
7. **Approval & Launch** — Obtain treasury, compliance, operations, technology, and audit sign-off.
8. **Periodic Review** — Revalidate each operating currency at least annually or when regulations change.

1. **Documentation** — Write clear SOP in Markdown
2. **Risk Assessment** — Identify and score risks
3. **Compliance Mapping** — Align with FATF, Basel, IFRS, local laws (OHADA, common law, etc.)
4. **Pilot Testing** — Run controlled pilot (30-90 days)
5. **Legal & Audit Review** — Obtain sign-off
6. **System Integration** — Add audit trails and logging
7. **Staff Training** — Roll out with training
8. **Monitoring & Annual Review**
