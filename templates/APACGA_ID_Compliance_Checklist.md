# APACGA ID Compliance Checklist

## KYC and CDD Controls

- [ ] Identity information is complete.
- [ ] Documents are current, legible, and independently verified.
- [ ] Beneficial owners and controllers are identified.
- [ ] Authorized signatories are supported by valid mandate evidence.
- [ ] Business purpose and expected activity are documented.

## AML/CFT and Sanctions Controls

- [ ] Sanctions screening completed.
- [ ] PEP screening completed.
- [ ] Adverse media screening completed.
- [ ] Internal fraud or blacklist screening completed.
- [ ] Potential matches are resolved before activation.
- [ ] True matches are escalated to compliance leadership.

## Risk and Approval Controls

- [ ] Risk matrix completed.
- [ ] Risk rating assigned.
- [ ] Enhanced due diligence completed for high-risk cases.
- [ ] Approval level matches risk classification.
- [ ] Exceptions are documented with expiry and owner.

## Privacy and Records Controls

- [ ] Lawful basis or consent is recorded.
- [ ] Evidence is stored in an approved secure repository.
- [ ] Access is restricted by role.
- [ ] Retention schedule is assigned.
- [ ] Audit log is complete.
