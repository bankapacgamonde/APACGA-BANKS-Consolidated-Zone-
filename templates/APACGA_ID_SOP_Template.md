## 2. Scope

This SOP applies to all APACGA ID requests, including customers, beneficial owners, authorized signatories, employees, vendors, agents, and other controlled counterparties.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Requester | Submits business purpose and initial identity information. |
| Operations maker | Creates or updates APACGA ID record and uploads evidence. |
| Compliance checker | Reviews KYC, CDD, screening, risk score, and exceptions. |
| Approver | Authorizes activation, changes, suspension, or closure. |
| Audit | Tests adherence to SOP and evidence completeness. |

## 4. Procedure

1. Receive APACGA ID request and assign case reference.
2. Check for duplicate APACGA ID records.
3. Complete KYC intake and collect required evidence.
4. Verify documents and record verification method.
5. Perform sanctions, PEP, adverse media, fraud, and internal screening.
6. Complete risk matrix and assign risk rating.
7. Escalate exceptions, true matches, high-risk cases, or incomplete evidence.
8. Obtain approvals according to the risk-based approval table.
9. Activate, suspend, reject, update, or close the APACGA ID.
10. Store evidence and update the audit log.

## 5. Exception Handling

Every exception must include the reason, compensating control, owner, approval, expiry date, and review date.

## 6. Records

Maintain APACGA ID records, evidence, screening results, approvals, and audit logs according to applicable retention requirements.
